Our purpose

To bring to light for everyone what is the plan of the mystery hidden for ages in God, who created all things.

Ephesians 3:9

To be the leading centre of hope, answers and lasting solutions for a world in need.

We exist to restore hope, provide answers and create lasting solutions, by raising people who are able to respond to the needs of their world.

Our pastor

Pastor Bankole Anifowose

Lead Pastor and Founder

Pastor Bankole leads with one conviction: every person is made for a purpose, and that purpose is found in the Word of God.

Every gathering, ministry and classroom flows from it, and from a deep love for the city we serve.

We move forward with clarity, purpose, and the unwavering commitment to raise people equipped to serve their world effectively.
